Diana Alina Serbanescu is a AI researcher, choreographer, and performance artist working at the intersection of embodiment, artificial intelligence, and feminist thought.
At ZHdK, she develops her work within Performance AI, a research project led by Chris Salter, focusing on choreographic systems that bring embodied practice, computation, and ethics into shared performative frameworks.
Diana's practice explores choreography as a site for negotiating agency between human and non-human actors. Through performance, sound, and wearable technologies, she creates poetic systems in which bodies and machines listen, co-compose, and share presence.
Situated between artistic research and systems design, her work investigates how agency and aliveness emerge across bodies, machines, and environments. She approaches performance as both method and site—a laboratory for rehearsing and reconfiguring human–machine relations.
Her current research includes Song Machines, a practice-based project exploring the voice as an embodied interface for human–machine co-performance, and [Embodied Synthesisers (2025-ongoing)](https://diananeranti.com/projects/embodied-synthesisers), which explores choreographic duets in which bodies negotiate balance through tensile, sensor-embedded membranes.
Her recent projects include [She Brings the Rain (Tanz im August, 2023)](https://diananeranti.com/projects/she-brings-the-rain) and [Embodied Voice and AI (2022)](https://diananeranti.com/projects/embodied-voice-and-ai). She is co-founder and artistic director of REPLICA.
